01 — About

About.

Steve N is a Latin-born, Geneva-based DJ and producer moving between house and indie dance: warm, rhythmic and made for the dancefloor.

Latest single “Low Effort” with Mert Erkan, out on Aqstica Audio, landed on the Spotify editorial playlists Umami, Electronic Rising, New Music Friday Türkiye and DANS 2026. Earlier releases “With You” (2.1M+ streams) and “Mirame” (250K+) have built an audience of 140K+ monthly listeners.

Behind the decks, Steve N has opened for Quevedo, Álvaro Díaz and Boza, played Corona Sunsets and Empire Music Festival in Guatemala, and earned support from Keinemusik, Notre Dame and Jamek Ortega.
